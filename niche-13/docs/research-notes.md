# Research notes — 29 September 2026

## Selection

“Best” here means five complementary jobs in a student's workflow, not a head-to-head benchmark. The examples avoid promises about grades, accuracy, unrestricted free access, and plan limits, which can change.

| Tool | Student job | Verified basis | Editorial qualification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ChatGPT Study mode | Work through a concept and self-test | [OpenAI Study mode guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11780217-using-study-mode-in-chatgpt) says it asks questions, explains step by step, and creates practice questions. | Can make mistakes; follow school AI rules. Plan limits still apply. |
| Google NotebookLM | Turn class materials into a study guide or audio review | [Google's NotebookLM overview](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/notebooklm-audio-overviews/) describes source-grounded answers, citations and Audio Overviews. | Output reflects uploaded sources and can omit or misread material. Check against originals. |
| Elicit | Find research papers for an assignment | [Elicit Paper Search](https://elicit.com/solutions/search) describes semantic paper search and research workflows. | Search results are leads, not a substitute for reading and citing the papers. |
| DeepL Write | Improve clarity and tone of a draft | [DeepL's Write guide](https://support.deepl.com/hc/en-us/articles/6318834492700-About-DeepL-Write) describes an AI writing assistant. | Keep the student's own argument and check what the edit changes. |
| Canva Magic Design | Build a presentation draft | [Canva Magic Design](https://www.canva.com/magic-design/) describes presentation design and limited uses on Canva Free. | Do not imply Canva Education is available to all students: [eligibility](https://www.canva.com/education/eligibility-guidelines/) excludes university students; school access has conditions. |

## Editorial checks

- Demonstrations should use fictional class materials and original visual recreations, not private student data or product screenshots presented as current UI.
- Show one short prompt and one useful result for each tool. Avoid generic feature lists.
- Use “five useful picks” in the narration rather than an absolute performance ranking.
- Tell viewers to verify citations and abide by their school's policy on AI use.
- Recheck feature availability, regional access and pricing at publication; these are deliberately absent from narration.
