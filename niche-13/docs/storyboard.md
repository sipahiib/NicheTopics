# Storyboard and narration draft

Target: exactly 180 seconds, 30 fps, 1920×1080. English narration, no burned-in subtitles. Original animated interface illustrations, with clear tool names. One shared master for both platforms. The current narration text is maintained in `../src/content.json`; the lines below describe each scene's editorial purpose.

| Time | Visual | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Five animated workflow cards introduce the sample assignment. | Establish five distinct student jobs. |
| 0:15–0:45 | ChatGPT Study mode: sample prompt, guiding question, and quiz cue appear. | Work through a concept with hints and a self-test. |
| 0:45–1:15 | NotebookLM: sample sources, study guide, and moving audio waveform appear. | Review uploaded class material and verify against originals. |
| 1:15–1:45 | Elicit: a research question produces three illustrative paper result cards. | Find academic leads and inspect original papers. |
| 1:45–2:15 | DeepL Write: the original paragraph and a clearer suggestion appear side by side. | Review edits without giving up the student's argument. |
| 2:15–2:45 | Canva Magic Design: a short outline produces three slide layouts. | Turn checked findings into an editable presentation. |
| 2:45–3:00 | Five workflow cards return. | Recap source checking and school AI rules. |

## Production notes

- Use one consistent sample assignment, such as “How does sleep affect learning?”, to connect all five demonstrations.
- Use a small on-screen label for each job: **Understand**, **Review**, **Research**, **Write**, **Present**.
- The tool scenes are explicitly labeled “illustrative demo.” The Elicit paper titles are fictional examples, not real citations.
- Music should leave speech intelligible. No unlicensed media or downloadable product logos are required.
