We use only the qualitative demand claim in narration.

All service packages are illustrative original scenarios, not client case studies. The $100 payment, $15 costs, five-hour delivery and $17/hour result are arithmetic assumptions, not advertised rates. Platform fees, taxes and acquisition time must be considered separately.
