# Research Notes

The script uses a balanced thesis: AI agents automate parts of software work, while present evidence does not support a simple “developers are finished” conclusion.

- Stack Overflow's 2025 survey reports that 46% of respondents distrust AI tool accuracy, compared with 33% who trust it. Only 3% report high trust. It also reports 87% concern about agent accuracy and 81% concern about security and privacy. [Source](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/ai)
- METR's randomized controlled trial covered 16 experienced open-source developers completing 246 tasks. With early-2025 AI tools, completion time increased by 19%. The study's scope and date are stated in the narration to avoid treating one result as universal. [Source](https://metr.org/Early_2025_AI_Experienced_OS_Devs_Study-paper.pdf)
- Google's 2025 DORA report describes AI as an amplifier of an organization's existing strengths and weaknesses. [Source](https://dora.dev/research/2025/dora-report/)
- The World Economic Forum lists software and application developers among the fastest-growing roles through 2030 and among roles contributing significant net job growth. These are employer projections, not guaranteed outcomes. [Source](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/2-jobs-outlook/)
